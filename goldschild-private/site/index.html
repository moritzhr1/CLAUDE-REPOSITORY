Overview

Corporate structure

Goldschild Private LLC is a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Florida and is privately held. Business correspondence is conducted by email and through the company’s registered mailing address in St. Petersburg, Florida.

Business model

The company’s business is the sale of consumer products for the home directly to private customers. Sales are made under the company’s own name through a proprietary online store built on the Shopify platform. Home safety is the initial product category.

Market focus

The company’s market is the European Union. Germany and Austria form its initial markets: two established e-commerce markets that share a common language, a common currency and the EU legal framework for consumer sales.
