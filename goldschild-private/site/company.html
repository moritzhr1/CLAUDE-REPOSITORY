The company

Retail, direct to the household.

Goldschild Private LLC is an e-commerce company organised in the State of Florida. Its model is direct-to-consumer retail: the company selects the products, offers them in its own online store and sells them directly to private customers.

01

What we’re building

Our own online store, built on Shopify and operated by Goldschild Private LLC. It is being set up now. Until it opens, there is no checkout and no orders can be placed.

The range will begin with a single subject: safety in the home. Product details, prices and terms of sale will be published in the store when it opens.

02

Who the store is for

Private customers: individuals and families buying for the home they live in. The store is a consumer store and is not aimed at trade or business buyers.

The range and the product information are being prepared with these customers in mind.

03

Launch markets

Germany and Austria. The store is being prepared for customers in these two countries only; no other markets are planned for the launch.

The company is registered in the United States; its customers will be in Europe. Delivery and payment terms for both countries will be published in the store when it opens.

About the name

Goldschild is German for “golden shield”. “Private” refers to private households, the customers the store is being built for. Goldschild Private LLC is a retailer: it is not a bank and does not offer investment or wealth management services.
