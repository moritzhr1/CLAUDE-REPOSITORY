Company

Company profile

An overview of the legal structure, business model, product focus and markets of Goldschild Private LLC, and of the regulatory framework in which the company conducts its business.

01

Corporate structure

Goldschild Private LLC is a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Florida, United States. The company is privately held.

Business correspondence is conducted by email and through the company’s registered mailing address in St. Petersburg, Florida.

02

Business model

The company’s business is direct-to-consumer e-commerce. Products are offered and sold through the company’s own online store, built on the Shopify platform, directly to private customers.

All sales are conducted under the company’s own name and through this single channel. The business model is based on the sale of goods; the company does not provide financial, banking or investment services.

03

Product focus

The company’s range consists of consumer products for the home. The initial product category is home safety.

The range is deliberately focused rather than broad. Products are selected for practical use in private households.

04

Markets

The company’s market is the European Union. Its initial markets are Germany and Austria.

Germany is one of the largest e-commerce markets in Europe. Together with Austria, it offers a common language, a common currency and a shared EU legal framework for consumer sales, conditions that allow both markets to be served through one consistent online store.

05

Regulatory framework

Sales to consumers in the European Union are subject to EU and national law, including the consumer protection rules for distance contracts, the EU General Product Safety Regulation and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The company’s sales model and online store are set up with the requirements for distance selling to consumers in Germany and Austria in mind.

About the name

The name combines the German word Goldschild, “golden shield”, with “Private”, which refers to the company’s customer segment: private consumers. Goldschild Private LLC is a commercial e-commerce company. It is not a bank and does not provide banking, investment or wealth management services.
